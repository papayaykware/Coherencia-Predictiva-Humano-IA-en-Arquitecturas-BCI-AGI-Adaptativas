# CPEA-TAE-FIELD v1.0 — preregistration

## Primary hypothesis
Structurally meaningful exceptions produce greater neurodynamic reconfiguration than physically matched but predictively irrelevant exceptions.

## Primary endpoint
Subject-level mean IRP contrast: meaningful_exception − irrelevant_exception.

## Secondary endpoints
Microstate transition-matrix Jensen-Shannon divergence; PLV change in theta/alpha/beta; permutation entropy; spectral entropy; Lempel-Ziv complexity; post-exception predictive accuracy or negative log likelihood.

## Fixed windows
Baseline −2.0:-0.2 s; immediate 0:1 s; reconfiguration 1:3 s; memory 3:8 s.

## Exception
Model surprise > 90th percentile of predictable-event surprise in an independent calibration set.

## TAE candidate
Surprise > 90th percentile AND IRP > 95th percentile of baseline/control calibration distribution AND persistence in the fixed reconfiguration window AND improved prediction of the altered sequence.

## Preprocessing
EEG >=250 Hz; band-pass 1–45 Hz; preregistered notch; fixed bad-channel and ICA/SSP criteria; no condition-specific preprocessing.

## Microstates
Primary k=4; polarity handling and clustering procedure frozen before confirmatory analysis; clustering/calibration independent from confirmatory epochs.

## Statistics
Primary two-sided alpha=.05, 95% CI, subject-level paired permutation test or preregistered mixed-effects model. Secondary endpoints FDR-corrected. At least 10,000 permutations.

## Surrogates
Phase randomization, trial-label permutation, and time scrambling where appropriate.

## Negative result
Failure of the primary contrast or failure in an independent cohort is failure to support the primary hypothesis.

## Interpretation boundary
Positive findings support neurodynamic reconfiguration only. They do not establish electromagnetic consciousness, causality, or quantum consciousness.
