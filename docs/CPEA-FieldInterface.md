# CPEA-FieldInterface

> Toward real-world AGI through coherence, anomaly learning and bioelectromagnetic contextual adaptation.

---

## Overview

CPEA-FieldInterface is an experimental research framework exploring the transition from conversational AGI systems toward physically contextualized adaptive intelligence.

The project integrates:

- EEG-driven coherence analysis
- DPCC (Post-Quantum Coherence Detection)
- TAE (Theory of Learning by Exception)
- Environmental electromagnetic signals
- Continual learning systems
- Symbolic-semantic adaptation
- Multimodal temporal embeddings
- Bioelectromagnetic interaction hypotheses
- METFI toroidal field modeling

The repository acts simultaneously as:

- experimental infrastructure,
- signal-processing framework,
- adaptive AGI laboratory,
- coherence analysis engine,
- multimodal cognition research environment.

---

# Core Hypothesis

Conventional language-based intelligence is insufficient for real-world adaptive cognition.

A physically contextualized AGI may require:

- temporal persistence,
- coherence perception,
- anomaly-based learning,
- environmental coupling,
- multimodal bioelectromagnetic integration,
- non-linear adaptive memory,
- signal-symbol interaction layers.

This repository investigates those requirements experimentally.

# Main Modules

## DPCC Engine

Detects coherence divergence and structural instability.

### Initial metrics

* spectral entropy
* synchronization
* coherence scoring
* phase locking
* fractal dynamics

---

## TAE Layer

Theory of Learning by Exception.

The system prioritizes:

* anomalies,
* discontinuities,
* rare events,
* coherence collapse,
* structural bifurcations.

Instead of learning mainly from statistical normality.

* geophysical systems,
* biological systems,

* environmental coupling.
